# Draft ACARA data request — not submitted

Source application page: https://acara.edu.au/contact-us/acara-data-access

Request a 2025 school-level NAPLAN results export for all NSW schools, with explicit permission to display the published school-level fields in Twohelixes' public school dataset and map. We need the released school means, not student records, suppressed values, school contact information or a top-schools subset.

Requested fields:
- ACARA SML school ID and head-campus relationship.
- Assessment year, tested year level and domain.
- Published school mean and national mean; published similar-background comparison mean and uncertainty bounds where available.
- Publication/suppression status and participation, where released.
- School name and profile year range to distinguish unavailable data from inapplicable year levels.

Purpose: make released NSW school results searchable geographically, with source links, clear coverage counts, and suppression preserved. Keep demographic and participation percentages separate from achievement. Any aggregate measure would disclose its method and coverage and would not be presented as an official ACARA ranking or an overall school-quality rating.

Preferred format: CSV or XLSX keyed by SML ID, year, domain and year level. The practical coverage target is published domain results for at least 80% of NSW head schools; identify schools for which results cannot be published rather than filling their values.

Please confirm the licence and permitted public-display terms, fees if any, and handling/storage requirements before supplying any restricted data. If this use cannot be licensed, please identify an appropriate published-data product or an approved access method for My School's released results.

Required before submission: applicant name, organisation details, contact details and authorised signatory. No agreement has been accepted and no request has been sent.
