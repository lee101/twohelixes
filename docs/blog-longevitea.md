# Longevitea: an open research dataset for what survives the cup

Longevitea is the companion research project for studying accessible botanical
infusions as measurable chemistry rather than as a list of miracle claims.

The open repository contains candidate plants, compound and nutrient provenance,
hot-water temperature matrices, safety gates, literature evidence tiers,
diversity-constrained blend simulations, validation tests, and an end-to-end
measurement pipeline:

- [Open-source repository](https://github.com/lee101/longevitea)
- [Public app.nz article](https://app.nz/blog/longevitea-hot-water-longevity-research)

The first measurement campaign compares green, white, and black tea, hibiscus,
lemon balm, ginger, and yerba mate across defined temperature/time conditions.
The important output is not a “perfect tea.” It is a reproducible table of what
survives the cup, how uncertain the measurement is, and which combinations are
worth testing next.
